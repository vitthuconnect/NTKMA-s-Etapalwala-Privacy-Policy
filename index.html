Privacy Policy

e-Tapalwala – Public Notifications and Document Delivery

Effective Date: 2 November 2026

Service: e-Tapalwala

Software Operator: Vitthu Connect

Purpose: Distribution of authorised public notices and official documents through WhatsApp.

This Privacy Policy explains how e-Tapalwala handles information when authorised personnel use the platform to distribute public announcements, government notices, meeting information and related documents, including PDF files, through WhatsApp.

The e-Tapalwala software is developed, operated and maintained by Vitthu Connect. The relevant government office or department is responsible for approving the content and deciding which notices and documents are authorised for distribution.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on how the Service is used, the following information may be processed:

  • Recipient WhatsApp phone numbers and available contact details.
  • Public announcements, government notices and meeting information.
  • PDF documents and other official attachments uploaded for distribution.
  • Message delivery status, timestamps and message identifiers.
  • Administrative user accounts, login details and access permissions.
  • Technical logs required for system maintenance and security.

The Service processes only information reasonably necessary for its intended notification and document-delivery purposes.

2. Purpose of Information Processing

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Sending authorised government public announcements.
  • Distributing official notices and circulars.
  • Sharing meeting schedules, invitations and related PDF documents.
  • Delivering administrative and public-service notifications.
  • Maintaining message delivery records and administrative logs.
  • Maintaining system security and troubleshooting technical issues.

e-Tapalwala is not intended for advertising, behavioural profiling or selling recipient contact information.

3. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

e-Tapalwala uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and/or its affiliates to deliver messages and documents.

When messages are transmitted, information such as recipient phone numbers, message content, attachments and delivery metadata may be processed by Meta to provide WhatsApp messaging services.

The platform may also use hosting, database, storage and infrastructure providers to maintain the Service. These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Public Notices and Document Distribution

The Service is intended for distributing information approved for public or administrative circulation, including government announcements, notices, meeting details and official PDF documents.

Recipients may save, download, forward or share documents received through WhatsApp. Once documents are delivered, e-Tapalwala may not be able to control their further distribution.

The responsible government office should review all content before distribution and ensure that confidential, classified or restricted information is not published through the Service.

5. Authorised Access and Responsibility

Only personnel authorised by the relevant government office should access e-Tapalwala and distribute official communications.

The government office is responsible for approving message content, recipient lists, communication schedules and the purpose of official notifications.

Vitthu Connect provides and maintains the software platform. It does not independently authorise government announcements or determine government policy.

6. Information Sharing

Information may be shared where necessary for operating the Service, including:

  • With Meta/WhatsApp for transmitting messages and attachments.
  • With hosting and infrastructure providers for system operation.
  • With authorised government personnel for administrative purposes.
  • With competent authorities where required by applicable law.
  • Where necessary to investigate security incidents or misuse.

Vitthu Connect does not sell personal information or recipient contact details.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, administrative verification, audit requirements and applicable legal obligations.

Message logs, uploaded documents and recipient details may have different retention periods depending on the requirements of the responsible government office.

When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted or anonymised, subject to applicable record-retention requirements.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational security measures are used to protect information processed through e-Tapalwala.

These measures may include:

  • Role-based access controls.
  • Authentication and restricted administrative access.
  • Secure connections for data transmission.
  • Access logs and system monitoring.
  • Regular technical maintenance and security improvements.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their login credentials and report suspected unauthorised access.

9. Recipient Rights and Requests

Recipients who receive an incorrect or unauthorised notification may contact the government office identified in the communication.

Where applicable, recipients may request information about the source of their contact details, the purpose of communication, or correction of inaccurate information.

10. Children's Information

e-Tapalwala is not specifically designed to collect personal information directly from children. The responsible government office should avoid including children's personal information in public notices unless legally authorised and appropriate safeguards are in place.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the Service, applicable laws or operational practices.

Updated versions will be published at the location where this Privacy Policy is made available, along with the revised effective date.
